Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

This policy explains how Satoru Tokuhisa, trading as interdisciplinary design lab, handles information on this site (apps.dangkang.com) and when you buy a direct copy here. How each app handles information is described in that app's own privacy policy.

1. Browsing this site

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. It is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

2. Information from direct purchases

Payments are processed by Link, Stripe's payment service. When you buy, we receive the following from Link:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your country or region, and the product, amount, and time of purchase

Card numbers and other payment details are handled by Link and never reach us. Link's privacy policy governs how payment information is handled.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver the app and contact you about your purchase
  • To answer questions and handle refunds
  • To keep transaction records required by law

4. Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except where required by law.

5. Retention

Transaction records are kept for the period required by tax and other laws, then deleted.

6. Access, correction, and deletion

To access, correct, or delete your information, contact us below. We will do so except for records we are required by law to keep.

7. Changes

This policy may change. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
